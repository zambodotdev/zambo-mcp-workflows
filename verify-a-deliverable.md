# Verify a deliverable

## What it does

Checks the receipts inside work someone handed you: a pull request, a report, a doc, a chat log. Every Zambo receipt in it gets verified live, and you get one line per receipt instead of clicking them one at a time.

## Paste this prompt into any MCP client connected to https://zambo.dev/mcp

```
Use Zambo to verify this deliverable. Here is the work: PASTE THE TEXT OR LINK. Find every Zambo receipt in it, check each one, and tell me which verified and which did not, with the verify link for each.
```

No AI handy? The same check runs in your browser at https://zambodotdev.github.io/receipt-scan/ (Receipt Scan), and one receipt at a time at https://zambo.dev/verify/.

## What you get back

One verdict per receipt: verified, or not found, each with its link. A verified receipt proves the saved result was not changed after the fact; it does not prove the answer was right, and this workflow will tell you that plainly.
