# Hand work to another AI

## What it does

Packages the current job, its results, and its receipts into one handover link. Whoever opens it, a person or another AI, gets the full record and can continue the work or check every piece of it.

## Paste this prompt into any MCP client connected to https://zambo.dev/mcp

```
Use Zambo to hand this work over. Package what we did, the results, and the receipts into one handover, addressed to: PASTE WHO IT IS FOR (a client, a teammate, or another AI). Give me the handover link.
```

## What you get back

One link. The receiver sees the work, the results, and the receipts behind them in one place, each receipt checkable at zambo.dev/verify. No screenshot threads, no "trust me", and no re-explaining finished work.
