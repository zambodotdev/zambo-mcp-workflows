# Remember across AIs

## What it does

Saves facts, decisions, and working context into Zambo's memory, which every AI you connect can read. Say it once in one AI; pick it up in another. This is the workflow behind moving between ChatGPT, Grok, and Claude without starting over.

## Paste this prompt into any MCP client connected to https://zambo.dev/mcp

```
Use Zambo to remember this for me across all my AIs: PASTE THE FACTS, DECISIONS, OR CONTEXT. Confirm what you saved. Later, in any AI, I will say "use Zambo to recall" and you should bring it back.
```

To continue in a different AI, open it and say: "Use Zambo to recall what I saved, then wrap up my session so I can keep working here."

## What you get back

A confirmation of what was stored, and a receipt link for the save. The memory is tied to your Zambo identity, not to one app, so it survives switching AIs, devices, and sessions.
